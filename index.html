✦ OBITUARY ✦

In Loving Memory

"She was sunshine wrapped in a little girl's body — radiant, warm, and impossible to forget."

With heavy hearts, we announce the passing of our beloved Sapphire Brooklynn Nicole Parker, who left this world too soon at the tender age of 10 on September 15, 2025. Born on June 20, 2015, in the vibrant city of Las Vegas, Nevada, Sapphire was a shining light in the lives of all who had the privilege to know her.

Sapphire was the cherished daughter of Angelina and Jackson Parker, and the loving sister to her six siblings: Anna-Grace, Savannah, Brynleigh, Connor, Skyleigh, and Olivia. Together, they created a bond that was unbreakable, filled with laughter, adventure, and the kind of love that only siblings can share. Sapphire's presence brought joy and warmth into their lives, and her memory will forever be etched in their hearts.

In addition to her immediate family, Sapphire is survived by her cousins, her beloved grandparents, and a host of aunts and uncles who loved her dearly. She is preceded in death by her cousins, Kristina, Victoria, Gabby, Amberlynn, and Jordan, who welcomed her with open arms in the embrace of eternity.

Sapphire was not only a daughter and sister; she was a friend, a confidant, and a spark of joy to all who encountered her. Her laughter was infectious, her spirit uplifting, and her kindness boundless. She had a unique ability to see the beauty in the world and share it with others, inspiring those around her to embrace life with open hearts.

Though her time with us was far too brief, Sapphire's impact was profound and lasting. She taught us the importance of love, family, and cherishing each moment. Her light will continue to shine brightly in our memories, illuminating the path of those who remain.

As we gather to celebrate her life, let us remember the joy she brought into our lives and the love she so freely gave. Sapphire will forever be loved, cherished, and missed beyond what words and actions could ever describe. May she rest in peace, knowing she is forever in our hearts.

In lieu of flowers, the family requests that donations be made to a charity of your choice in Sapphire's memory, honoring her spirit of kindness and love.

A private viewing/funeral and celebration of life will take place on September 22, 2025, for friends and family.

✦ MEMORIES ✦

Treasured Moments

A collection of the moments that made Sapphire's life so beautiful

🎨

The Little Artist

Sapphire discovered face painting at age 5 and never looked back. She would spend hours creating elaborate unicorns, butterflies, and galaxies on anyone willing to sit still. Her face painting kit was her most prized possession.

🌈

Rainbow Chaser

After every rain shower, Sapphire would race outside looking for rainbows. She believed each rainbow was a message from the sky, and she kept a journal of every rainbow she ever spotted — 47 in total.

🦄

Unicorn Believer

Sapphire never stopped believing in magic. Her room was filled with unicorn plushies, rainbow crystals, and fairy lights. She told everyone that unicorns were real — they just chose who could see them.

💃

Born to Dance

From the moment she could walk, Sapphire danced. In the kitchen, in the grocery store, in the middle of the park — any music was an invitation. She had rhythm in her soul and joy in every step.

📚

Story Weaver

Sapphire had an extraordinary imagination. She would create entire worlds with her toys, complete with characters, storylines, and dramatic plot twists. Her family still has recordings of her elaborate storytelling sessions.

🌸

Garden Angel

Every spring, Sapphire would help plant flowers in the garden. She had a special gift with plants — everything she touched seemed to bloom more beautifully. She loved picking wildflowers and making bouquets for her mom.

"She didn't just live — she sparkled."

🖨️ PRINTING TIPS

📄 Memorial Card: Print on white or ivory cardstock (80–110 lb). Cut along the border. Laminate for durability.

📋 Laminated Obituary: Print on glossy photo paper (4×6 or 5×7). Take to a print shop for lamination.

🎨 Best results: Select "High Quality" or "Best" in your printer settings and enable background graphics.
